Our Story

Founded to Build What Others Only Imagine

Logik Structures, Inc. (LOGIK) was formed in 2015 with a clear purpose: to serve the structural engineering needs of homeowners, real estate developers, architects, and contractors developing enduring, cost-effective structures for the most ambitious architectural designs Southern California has to offer.

The firm is composed of professionals who have successfully delivered complex construction projects across the region. We bring to every assignment the rigor and leadership experience of careers spent at some of Southern California's most respected structural engineering firms.

In every project, we work side by side with all team players — from early design concept through project completion — to ensure the architectural design intent is realized in a practical, buildable, and cost-conscious manner.

Our Process

We Build It Virtually Before You Build It for Real

Our signature approach: every project is fully built in 3D — in extreme detail — before a single permit drawing is issued. This means structural conflicts, coordination issues, and constructability problems are resolved at the desk, not in the field.

The result: fewer RFIs, fewer change orders, and structures that perform exactly as designed from the first day of construction to the last.
