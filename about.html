About LOGIK

Enduring structures for intriguing designs.

Since 2015 our line has been the same: where enduring and cost-effective structures meet intriguing and sustainable designs.

01 The office

Founded to build what others only imagine.

Logik Structures, Inc. was formed in 2015 to serve homeowners, real estate developers, architects and contractors who are developing enduring, cost-effective structures for the most ambitious architecture Southern California has to offer.

The firm is made up of professionals who have delivered complex construction projects across the region, and who bring the rigour and leadership of careers spent at some of Southern California's most respected structural engineering firms.

On every project we work side by side with the whole team, from early concept to completion, so the architectural intent is realised in a practical, buildable and cost-conscious way.

Sound structural engineering practices, melded with a firm commitment to project quality, are the key to the success of each assignment.

— LOGIK core principle
02 Method

We build it virtually before you build it for real.

Every project is fully built in 3D, in extreme detail, before a single permit drawing is issued.

Structural conflicts, coordination issues and constructability problems are resolved at the desk rather than in the field. The result is fewer RFIs, fewer change orders, and structures that perform as designed from the first day of construction to the last.

03 Why clients return
  1. 01

    We coordinate, not just calculate

    The work doesn't end at the calculation sheet. We stay engaged with architects, contractors and inspectors until the last bolt is tightened.

  2. 02

    Cost is part of the brief

    Every structural decision carries a price tag. We design for material efficiency and constructability from day one, so elegance and economy aren't competing goals.

  3. 03

    No surprises in the field

    Conflicting conditions, missing details and coordination gaps are resolved before ground is broken. What gets built matches what was designed.
