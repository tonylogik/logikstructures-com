About LOGIK

Built on Experience.
Driven by Precision.

Logik Structures, Inc. was founded in 2015 with a single mandate: deliver structural engineering that is as precise as it is practical. Our team brings over 20 years of combined experience managing complex construction projects across Southern California.

Every project starts in 3D. We virtually build the entire structure in Tekla or Revit before a single permit drawing is issued — eliminating constructability problems before they become field problems. The result: fewer RFIs, smoother construction, and structures that perform exactly as designed.
