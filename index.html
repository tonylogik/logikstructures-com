04 Method

Every building gets built twice. The first time, nobody gets a change order.

  1. 01 — Concept

    Find the structure inside the idea

    We join at the sketch stage and work beside the architect, so the engineering supports the design intent instead of fighting it.

  2. 02 — Model

    Build it virtually, in detail

    The full structure goes into Tekla or Revit. Conflicts and coordination gaps are resolved at a desk, not on a slab.

  3. 03 — Permit

    Issue drawings that get built

    Coordinated, permit-ready structural packages, with the architectural and MEP consultants already reconciled.

  4. 04 — Build

    Stay until it's finished

    Construction administration, RFI responses and submittal review, through to project completion.
