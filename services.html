Scope of work

Structure, from footing to field.

Four disciplines under one roof, all run through the same 3D-first workflow. What you get, and when to call, is listed below.

S1
New construction

Built right from day one

Complete structural engineering for new residential and commercial buildings — from single-family custom homes to multi-storey commercial developments. We work with architects and developers to turn ambitious designs into sound, permit-ready documents.

The whole structural system is built virtually before construction begins, which removes field conflicts and protects the schedule.

Included
  • Foundation design — shallow, deep and hillside systems
  • Gravity and lateral systems in wood, steel and concrete
  • 3D structural modelling in Tekla Structures and Autodesk Revit
  • Coordination with architectural and MEP consultants
  • Permit-ready structural drawing packages
  • Construction administration and RFI response
Call us whenYou have a site and a concept — ideally before the floor plans are frozen. Early structure is cheap structure.
S2
Remodel & addition

Respect for what's there. Room for what's next.

Remodels mean working within, and around, what already exists. We investigate and analyse the existing conditions first, then engineer modifications that are code-compliant and cost-effective.

Removing a bearing wall, adding a second storey or opening a floor plan gets the same rigour we bring to a new building.

Included
  • Load-bearing wall removals and beam design
  • Second-storey additions and vertical expansions
  • Open-plan conversions, residential and commercial
  • Foundation assessment and underpinning design
  • Room additions and accessory dwelling units (ADUs)
  • Existing-condition documentation and structural assessment
Call us whenSomeone says "that wall can probably come out." It probably can. It needs a beam, a load path and a permit first.
S3
Seismic evaluation & retrofit

Protecting what already stands

Southern California sits across major fault systems. Older buildings — especially those designed before the code changes that followed the Northridge earthquake — often carry weaknesses that stay invisible until the ground moves.

We evaluate existing buildings and design targeted retrofits: residential soft-story work, commercial assessments and school facilities.

Included
  • Seismic vulnerability assessments (ASCE 41, CBC)
  • Soft-story and weak-story retrofit design
  • Concrete and masonry building evaluations
  • School and essential-facility retrofit engineering
  • Retrofit cost-benefit analysis
  • Coordination with building departments and inspectors
Call us whenYou've received an order to comply, you're buying an older building, or a lender or insurer is asking questions.
S4
Shop drawing production

Precise documents. No field ambiguity.

Shop drawings are the last check between design intent and what actually gets fabricated. We produce coordinated packages for rebar, structural steel and HVAC supports, reviewed for constructability and code compliance.

Because the structure already exists in our model, the shop drawings inherit its detail and accuracy.

Included
  • Rebar shop drawing production and review
  • Structural steel fabrication drawings
  • HVAC structural support and hanger details
  • Constructability review and RFI prevention
  • Coordination with fabricators and contractors
  • Submittal review and engineer-of-record responses
Call us whenYou're a contractor or fabricator who needs drawings that fit first time, on a programme that has no room for a second try.
FAQ Plain answers

Questions we hear on the first call.

General guidance only — every building and jurisdiction is different, and we're glad to look at yours.

When do I actually need a structural engineer?

Whenever the work changes how a building carries load: removing or altering a bearing wall, adding a storey or an addition, building on a slope, cutting large openings, or building new. For work like this the building department will generally ask for structural drawings and calculations as part of the permit.

What is a soft-story building, and is mine one?

A soft story is a weak, open level — usually ground-floor tuck-under parking — beneath stiffer floors above. In an earthquake that open level can rack sideways. The City of Los Angeles adopted a mandatory retrofit ordinance in 2015 for certain wood-frame buildings built under pre-1978 codes, and neighbouring cities such as Santa Monica and West Hollywood run their own programmes. If you have received a notice, send it to us and we'll tell you what it requires.

Why are hillside projects more involved?

The ground is doing as much work as the building. Hillside lots usually need retaining structures, temporary shoring during excavation and deeper foundations, and the construction sequence matters. On several of our hillside projects we have served as both structural and shoring engineer, so the two designs are worked out together.

What do you need from me to prepare a proposal?

The site address, whatever drawings or sketches exist, a soils report if you have one, and your target schedule. If there are no drawings yet, a conversation and a few photos is enough to start.

What does "3D-first" change for me?

The complete structure is modelled in detail before permit drawings are issued, so clashes with architecture, ducts and plumbing show up on a screen. In practice that means fewer RFIs, fewer change orders and fewer days lost on site.

Where do you work?

Greater Los Angeles and the wider Southern California region. Projects on this site are in Los Angeles, West Hollywood, El Segundo, Pasadena and Century City, among others.
