Structural Engineering
From the Ground Up

LOGIK delivers precise structural engineering across four core disciplines — every one built on the same commitment to quality, coordination, and constructability.

01 — New Construction

Built Right from Day One

LOGIK provides comprehensive structural engineering services for new residential and commercial construction — from single-family custom homes to multi-story commercial developments. We work with architects and developers to translate ambitious designs into structurally sound, permit-ready documents.

Our 3D-first workflow means the complete structural system is built virtually before construction begins, eliminating field conflicts and keeping your project on schedule.

  • Foundation design — shallow, deep, and hillside systems
  • Gravity and lateral system development (wood, steel, concrete)
  • 3D structural modeling in Tekla Structures and Autodesk Revit
  • Full coordination with architectural and MEP consultants
  • Permit-ready structural drawing packages
  • Construction administration and RFI response

02 — Remodeling & Renovation

Respect for What's There.
Room for What's Next.

Remodeling projects present a unique structural challenge: working within — and around — what already exists. LOGIK specializes in understanding existing conditions through investigation and analysis, then engineering modifications that are both code-compliant and cost-effective.

Whether it's removing a load-bearing wall, adding a second story, or opening up a floor plan, we bring the same rigor to renovation work that we bring to new construction.

  • Load-bearing wall removals and structural beam design
  • Second-story additions and vertical expansions
  • Open-plan conversions for residential and commercial spaces
  • Foundation assessments and underpinning design
  • Room additions and accessory dwelling units (ADUs)
  • Existing condition documentation and structural assessment

03 — Seismic Evaluation & Retrofit

Protecting What Already Stands

Southern California sits at the intersection of major fault systems. Existing structures — particularly older buildings designed to pre-Northridge code standards — often carry seismic vulnerabilities that are invisible until they're not. LOGIK provides rigorous seismic evaluations and designs targeted retrofit strategies to address those vulnerabilities before they become emergencies.

Our seismic work spans residential soft-story retrofits, commercial building assessments, and school facility retrofits — each approached with the same analytical depth and commitment to protecting lives and property.

  • Seismic vulnerability assessments (ASCE 41, CBC)
  • Soft-story and weak-story retrofit design
  • Concrete and masonry building evaluations
  • School and essential facility retrofit engineering
  • Retrofit cost-benefit analysis
  • Coordination with building departments and inspectors

04 — Shop Drawing Production

Precision Documents.
Zero Field Ambiguity.

Shop drawings are the last line of defense between design intent and what actually gets built. LOGIK produces detailed, coordinated shop drawing packages for rebar, structural steel, and HVAC systems — reviewed for constructability and code compliance before they ever reach the fabricator or contractor.

Our shop drawing work is a direct extension of our 3D modeling capabilities. Because we build the structure in the model first, our shop drawings carry the same level of detail and coordination accuracy that characterizes all LOGIK deliverables.

  • Rebar shop drawing production and review
  • Structural steel fabrication drawings
  • HVAC structural support and hanger details
  • Constructability review and RFI prevention
  • Coordination with fabricators and contractors
  • Submittal review and engineer-of-record responses
